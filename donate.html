Enlight Mission works with Irula tribal families in Villupuram district, Tamil Nadu. We obtain the legal documents that give them access to what the state already owes them, and we run a tailoring programme that lets women earn their own income. The work is small, local and countable — which is what makes it straightforward to fund and to report against.

What has been done so far

Enlight Mission has run continuously in Alampoondi since 2016.

  • 3000 caste certificates obtained for Irula people
  • 700 land grants currently being processed
  • 100 women taught tailoring since the programme began in April 2016
  • 45 women currently following the tailoring programme

Alongside these: goat rearing for household livelihood, house openings, ration cards and voter ID registration. All four are documented in photographs on the projects page.

Ways a company can fund the work

Each of these is a distinct programme with its own costs and its own reporting.

Legal documentation drive

Caste certificates, land grant confirmation and scheme cards. Our founder is a practising lawyer; this is the work that unlocks government entitlements the families are already due.

Cost per certificate — on request

The tailoring programme

Daily courses at the Rakhi Community College in Alampoondi. Six months to a year per student. Graduates can make professional clothing, and can rent a machine to teach others at home.

Annual programme cost — on request

Sewing machines

A discrete, countable unit of support. Machines stay with the programme and are rented out to graduates, so each one keeps working after the first student finishes.

Cost per machine — on request

Goat rearing for livelihood

Livestock placed with Irula households so a family has an asset of its own to raise and sell. Photographs of past distributions are on the projects page.

Cost per household — on request

Employee engagement

Company teams are welcome to visit the village and the community college, meet the women in the programme, and see the work directly. Volunteers already support us in administration, teaching and documentation.
