About Us

#Page en français

Enlight Mission is an NGO based in Tamil Nadu, India, that supports local tribal communities living in poverty. Our mission is to enlight darkness of poverty in Irulas livelihood and support them in building their awareness how to live better.

Albert Velankanni is the founder of Enlight. As a lawyer he helps Irulas with legal documents to make them benefit of the government scheme.

The Rakhi Foundation founded by Danielle Brochier is our sponsor and partner :
