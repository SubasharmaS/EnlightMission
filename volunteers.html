Volunteer

#Page en français

Volontees are more than welcome. You can share your ideas and support Irulight Assotiation in many areas

  • Share your ideas

    Find your area of interest, develop your passion by your work for Assotiation and grow together with us!

  • Financial support

    What we do, we do from our hearts. Social work is not business, but sometimes we need to collect some money to be able to still support Irulas. Your donation is ore then appreciated. Please share our needs with your friends!

  • Administration

    Like every organisation we have to manage documents, papers, registration books and software files — we need some well-organised people to help us with that!

  • Social network

    Tell your friends about Irulight, show your work in your counrty and help us to encourage new volunteers to work with us!

  • Social media

    Facebook, Instagram, YouTube, Crowdfounfing, WWW — support us in showing us to the world!

  • Video

    Show the life of the tribe using your creativity! Support us in creating our YouTube channel.

  • Wordpress

    Help us in promotion og our mission by creating website of our Assotiation!

  • Acknowledge tribes

    You can help us Show the life of the tribe using your creativity! knowing more about the Irula’s, by going to the village and gather informations about them, e.g: the number of people in the village, what do they like to do or would like to do in the future in their life, what they need, and problems they are facing.

  • Teaching

    You can teach your skills to other people working in the Association or in schools nearby. It can be in informatics, any social idea, or any ideas for how to make an association something great!
