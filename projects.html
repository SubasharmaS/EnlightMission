Our Work & Projects

Goat Rearing Project

Goats given to Irula families so they have livestock of their own to raise and sell.

House Opening

Permanent houses handed over to families whose mud homes were destroyed by the monsoon.

Ration Card Issuing

Ration cards obtained for families, giving them access to the government food scheme.

Voter Id Issuing

Voter IDs registered so Irula people can take part in elections for the first time.
