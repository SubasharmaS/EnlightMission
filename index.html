Enlighting the darkness of poverty in Irula livelihood

Enlight Mission is an NGO based in Tamil Nadu, India, that supports education and economic and social development for women and local tribal communities.

See how you can help About Enlight Mission

What we have achieved

  • 3000 caste certificates obtained for Irula people
  • 700 land grants currently being processed
  • 100 women taught tailoring since the programme started
  • 45 women currently following the tailoring programme

What we do

Four programmes, one aim — that Irula families get what they are owed

Empowering and Enlighting Women Tailoring courses every day since April 2016, giving women the skills to earn their own money. Read more
Irula Tribes Development Caste certificates, land grants and scheme cards for a community cut off from the benefits it is entitled to. Read more
Projects Goat rearing, house openings, ration cards and voter IDs — the practical work, in photographs. Read more
Volunteers Nine ways to help, from teaching and administration to video and social media. Read more
